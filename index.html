    🚀 Hyper-Surge: Multiplier Overdrive

    A fast-paced neon arcade game where your score is driven by 6+ stacking, compounding multiplier systems. Rather than simple flat points, your score scales exponentially as you chain combos, execute ricochet trick shots, brave high-voltage danger zones, and trigger supernova surges.

    ⚡ The Multiplier Mathematical Engine

    The core scoring formula calculates the point value for every target destroyed via multiplicative compounding:

    $$\text{Final Score} = \text{Base Points} \times (M_{\text{base}} \times M_{\text{combo}} \times M_{\text{zone}} \times M_{\text{ricochet}} \times M_{\text{precision}} \times M_{\text{harmony}} \times M_{\text{powerup}})$$

    1. ⚡ Combo Velocity Multiplier ($M_{\text{combo}}$)

    • Range: $1.0\times \to 10.0\times$ (up to $15.0\times$ with upgrades)
    • Mechanism: Each hit adds $+0.25\times$ to your combo streak.
    • Decay Gauge: A HUD timer bar empties over 3.5 seconds. Hitting any target refreshes the timer. If it runs out, the combo resets.

    2. 🌀 Danger Vortex Multiplier ($M_{\text{zone}}$)

    • Range: $1.8\times \to 4.0\times$
    • Mechanism: Dynamic shifting vortex fields drift across the arena:
    • Red Vortex Core: $2.5\times$ multiplier
    • Gold Flux Conduit: $1.8\times$ multiplier
    • Neutral Grid: $1.0\times$
    • Standing inside a danger zone multiplies all points scored while inside, rewarding high-risk positioning over safe camping.

    3. 🔄 Ricochet Wall Bounces ($M_{\text{ricochet}}$)

    • Range: $+0.5\times \to +1.0\times$ per bounce
    • Mechanism: Your energy pulses bounce up to 2 times off the arena perimeter. Hitting targets with deflected shots rewards trick-shot multipliers!

    4. 🎯 Precision Bullseye Multiplier ($M_{\text{precision}}$)

    • Range: $2.0\times \to 3.5\times$
    • Mechanism: Every target has a glowing center core. Striking within $35\%$ of the target's center triggers a golden critical explosion with high-pitch synthesizer chimes.

    5. 💎 Color Harmony Chain ($M_{\text{harmony}}$)

    • Range: $1.0\times \to 4.0\times$
    • Mechanism: Targets spawn in 3 radiant frequencies (Frost Cyan, Void Magenta, Solar Amber). Destroying matching colors in sequence scales your harmony chain. Hitting rare Rainbow Prisms awards an instant $4.0\times$ Harmony boost!

    6. 🚀 Overdrive Surge Cores ($M_{\text{powerup}}$)

    • Surge Types:
    • ⚡ Quantum Surge (2.0x): 8 seconds of amplified velocity.
    • ⏳ Chrono Stasis (3.0x): Slows enemies down with a 3x multiplier.
    • 🔥 Supernova Frenzy (5.0x): Screen shake, bass drops, and massive rainbow aura.

    🛠️ Multiplier Overclock Perk Shop

    Eliminating targets with high active multipliers extracts Multiplier Shards ($\lfloor \text{Multiplier} / 5 \rfloor$), which can be permanently spent on:

    1. Base Multiplier Booster: Starts every run at $+0.25\times$ higher baseline.
    2. Combo Grace Capacitor: Extends the combo decay timer by $+0.8\text{s}$ per level.
    3. Bullseye Precision Core: Increases Critical hit multiplier by $+0.75\times$.
    4. Danger Vortex Resonator: Amplifies Danger Zone multiplier by $+0.5\times$.
    5. Ricochet Overdrive: Adds $+0.5\times$ extra score per wall deflection.
    6. Supernova Prolong: Extends powerup durations by $+2.5\text{s}$.

    🔊 Procedural Audio Synthesis

    Hyper-Surge uses the native Web Audio API with zero external audio assets or downloads: - Pitch-Scaling Hits: Hit chimes dynamically shift pitch higher in real time as your multiplier climbs ($1\times \to 100\times+$)! - Harmonic Chimes: Distinct crystalline tones for critical bullseyes and powerup pickups. - Cyberpunk Synth Bassline: An integrated procedural 16th-note rhythm beat that reacts to gameplay.
